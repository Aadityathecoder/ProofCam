# Research evidence

These reports retain historical desktop and iPhone measurements. During the October 26, 2026 iPhone scope cleanup, obsolete platform-specific reports and zero-count fields were removed, and limitation wording was updated. Numeric measurements were not rerun or promoted to device qualification.

Recorded source, plan and report hashes describe the original experimental snapshots; they are not hashes of the edited checkout. Existing locked plans must not be silently regenerated or rehashed. New runs require a new versioned plan and output directory. The locked corpus manifests and dataset attribution are unchanged.

The current cross-decoder tool checks Python and Swift. Historical iPhone parity reports also include a third decoder that is no longer part of the project.
